The Story

A civilisation forged in metal.

The Indian subcontinent has a long record of mining, smelting, alloying, casting and forging. From early copper working to crucible steel and zinc distillation, metallurgical practice developed through generations of observation and craft.

DHĀTU brings these traditions together as a readable research archive, giving visitors a place to explore the materials, processes and historical evidence behind them.

“The history of a civilisation can often be read in the materials it learned to transform.”
